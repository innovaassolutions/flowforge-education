**B. Academic Satisfaction**

  

- Perceived teaching quality
    

  

- Student academic progress
    

  

- Homework management
    

  

- Assessment transparency
    

  

- Support for learning differences
    

  

**C. Safety & Wellbeing**

  

- Physical safety perception
    

  

- Bullying handling
    

  

- Social-emotional support
    

  

- Digital safety
    

  

**D. School Culture**

  

- Inclusiveness
    

  

- Warmth of environment
    

  

- How welcome parents feel
    

  

- School-parent partnership
    

  

**E. Operations & Services**

  

- Transport reliability
    

  

- Cafeteria quality
    

  

- CCA/after-school programs
    

  

- Administrative responsiveness
    

**F. Psychological & Emotional Indicators**

- “I feel my child receives enough attention.”
    

**A. Communication Clarity**

  

(We expand this because communication is the #1 complaint in private/intl schools.)

  

- How clear are the school’s announcements regarding schedules, deadlines, and events?
    

  

- Does the school use too many communication channels (apps, emails, portals), causing confusion?
    

  

- Are communications consistent across departments (e.g., admissions vs academics vs finance)?
    

  

- When important information changes, does the school notify parents promptly?
    

  

- Do you feel overwhelmed by the volume of school communication?
    

  

- Are instructions for school events, dress codes, assessments, etc., easy to follow?
    

  

- Are communications written in a professional and respectful tone?
    

  

- Do you trust that the school tells parents everything they need to know? (powerful psychological indicator)
    

  

**B. Frequency & Quality of Updates**

  

- How often do you receive updates about your child’s academic progress?
    

  

- Do you feel the school communicates “too much”, “too little”, or “just right”?
    

  

- Do teachers provide meaningful feedback, not just generic comments?
    

  

- Does the school send reminders ahead of important deadlines/events?
    

  

- Are weekly/monthly newsletters helpful or overwhelming?
    

  

- Are key dates communicated early enough for you to plan ahead?
    

  

- Does the school keep you informed about changes in teachers or staff turnover?
    

  

**C. Access to Teachers & Admin**

  

- This is where MOST parent anger actually lives.
    

  

- How easy is it to contact teachers when needed?
    

  

- How reasonable is the response time from teachers? (within 24 hours? 48 hours?)
    

  

- Are teachers open to feedback, or do they seem defensive?
    

  

- Do you feel comfortable raising concerns with teachers?
    

  

- How easy is it to contact administrative staff (accounts, transport, admissions, etc.)?
    

  

- How efficiently did admin resolve an issue you faced this year?
    

  

- Were you ever passed around between multiple staff to solve a simple problem?
    

  

- Do you feel your concerns are taken seriously?
    

  

- Are staff respectful and professional in their communication with parents?
    

  

- Has any staff member made you feel dismissed or belittled?
    

  

**D. Two-Way Communication Quality (Responsiveness)**

  

- How quickly does the school respond to emails?
    

  

- Does leadership respond personally to important matters?
    

  

- Are there accessible channels for constructive feedback?
    

  

- If you escalate a concern, does it get addressed effectively?
    

  

- Do you feel the school genuinely listens to parent perspectives?
    

  

- Does the principal or vice-principal make themselves available to parents?
    

  

- Have you ever felt stonewalled or ignored by leadership/admin?
    

  

- Are school decisions explained clearly with context (not vague or PR-style)?
    

  

- Do you trust the school’s communication to be honest, not performative?
    

  

**E. Transparency (Policies, Tuition, Leadership Decisions)**

  

THIS is where many schools fail horribly.

  

- Are tuition fee increases explained clearly and justified?
    

  

- Are school policies (discipline, homework, grading, absences) easy to access and understand?
    

  

- Are teacher qualifications clearly published and accessible?
    

  

- Does the school communicate openly about teacher turnover?
    

  

- Are major policy changes announced early and with rationale?
    

  

- Do you feel the school hides information or avoids accountability?
    

  

- Does the school clearly outline what your tuition covers (value clarity)?
    

  

- Is financial communication clear and consistent (invoices, fees, breakdowns)?
    

  

- Are decisions that affect students (new rules, major changes) explained thoroughly?
    

  

**F. Academic Satisfaction & Curriculum Fit**

  

- Do you feel teachers are qualified and skilled in their subjects?
    

  

- Is your child appropriately challenged academically?
    

  

- Are you satisfied with the curriculum offered (IB, British, IGCSE, A Levels, etc.)?
    

  

- Does the curriculum workload feel healthy or overwhelming?
    

  

- Does the curriculum prepare your child for future steps (university, next stage)?
    

  

- Are you satisfied with the school’s approach to languages, arts, sports?
    

  

- Does the school identify your child’s learning needs early?
    

  

- Does the school support your child if they struggle?
    

  

- Are high achievers challenged sufficiently?
    

  

**G. Wellbeing & Safety**

  

Does your child feel safe at school?

  

- How effectively does the school handle bullying?
    

  

- Does the school communicate wellbeing issues promptly?
    

  

- Do you trust the safeguarding procedures?
    

  

- Do you feel the school provides adequate emotional support?
    

  

- Has the school ever mishandled a wellbeing or disciplinary case?
    

  

- Do you feel your child is known personally, not just a number?
    

  

**H. School Culture & Community**

  

- Do you feel welcomed as a parent at this school?
    

  

- Is the school inclusive of diverse backgrounds and cultures?
    

  

- Is the parent community respectful and supportive?
    

  

- Do you feel the school values parent partnership?
    

  

- I. Operations & Services (Transport, Cafeteria, Admin)
    

  

- Is school transport reliable and safe?
    

  

- Is the cafeteria food nutritious and reasonably priced?
    

  

- Are school facilities clean and well-maintained?
    

  

- Does the admin office resolve issues efficiently?
    

  

- Is billing accurate and communicated clearly?
    

  

⭐ **J. Emotional & Psychological Indicators (DEEP SIGNALS)**

  

These are the ones that reveal the TRUE health of the school-parent relationship.

  

- “I feel respected as a parent by this school.”
    

  

- “I trust the school leadership team.”
    

  

- “I feel the school understands my child’s needs.”
    

  

- “I feel anxious dealing with the school/admin.”
    

  

- “This school values parent input.”
    

  

- “If something serious happened, I trust the school to handle it well.”
    

  

- “The school communicates openly and transparently.”
    

  

- “I feel confident recommending this school to friends.” (NPS indicator)
