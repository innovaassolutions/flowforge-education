**🌋 A. Workload, Burnout & Time Allocation**

  

- Is your weekly workload manageable?
    

  

- How many hours per week do you spend on lesson planning?
    

  

- How many hours per week do you spend on admin duties?
    

  

- Do you regularly take work home?
    

  

- Are class sizes appropriate for the level you teach?
    

  

- Do you feel you have enough time to give quality feedback to students?
    

  

- How often do you experience burnout symptoms?
    

  

- Do you feel pressure from leadership to “do more with less”?
    

  

- How sustainable does your workload feel long-term?
    

  

**📘 B. Curriculum Delivery, Teaching Style & Academic Expectations**

  

- Do you have autonomy in choosing your teaching style?
    

  

- Is your teaching approach aligned with the curriculum (IB, IGCSE, A Levels, etc.)?
    

  

- Does the curriculum provide enough flexibility for student needs?
    

  

- Are learning objectives clearly defined by the school?
    

  

- Do you feel confident delivering your subject based on provided materials?
    

  

- How often do you assign homework? Is this expectation manageable for students?
    

  

- How do you measure student progress, and is this method supported by the school?
    

  

- Do you feel pressured to teach “to the test”?
    

  

- Does leadership understand the realities of your curriculum?
    

  

**🧠 C. Classroom Management, Discipline & Student Behaviour**

  

- Are school disciplinary policies clear and consistently enforced?
    

  

- Do you feel supported by leadership when handling misbehavior?
    

  

- How often do you handle bullying incidents in your class?
    

  

- Are you satisfied with the tools/resources available for behavior management?
    

  

- How do you deal with disruptive students?
    

  

- Does the school back you up when parents challenge disciplinary actions?
    

  

- Do you feel student behavior has improved, worsened, or stayed the same in the last year?
    

  

- Is there a culture of respect between students and teachers?
    

  

**👥 D. Teacher–Teacher Relationship Dynamics (Peer Culture)**

  

- Do you feel supported by your colleagues?
    

  

- Is there collaboration within your department?
    

  

- Is there collaboration ACROSS departments?
    

  

- Do you feel isolated in your work?
    

  

- Are there cliques or factions among staff that affect your work?
    

  

- Do you feel competition or cooperation is more dominant?
    

  

- Does gossip or politics interfere with teaching culture?
    

  

- “I feel safe sharing ideas and concerns with colleagues.”
    

  

- THIS section reveals toxic culture instantly.
    

  

**🧑‍💼 E. Teacher–Leadership Relationship**

  

- Does leadership communicate expectations clearly?
    

  

- Do you feel micromanaged or trusted?
    

  

- Does leadership actively support your professional growth?
    

  

- Does the principal/SLT understand classroom realities?
    

  

- Are teacher voices included in school decisions that affect teaching?
    

  

- Do you feel leadership is transparent in its decisions?
    

  

- Do you trust leadership to act in the best interest of teachers?
    

  

- If you raised a concern, would leadership take it seriously?
    

  

- Have you ever felt intimidated or silenced by leadership?
    

  

_This section is CRITICAL — many teachers leave because of leadership, not students._

  

**🧩 F. Professional Development & Career Growth**

  

- Are PD sessions relevant to your level/subject?
    

  

- Are PD opportunities fair and accessible to all teachers?
    

  

- Does PD feel useful or mandatory “box ticking”?
    

  

- Do you feel the school invests in your long-term development?
    

  

- Do you see a clear growth path within the school?
    

  

- Do you feel motivated to improve your practice?
    

  

- Would you stay longer if career growth opportunities improved?
    

  

💼 G. Administrative & Operational Support

  

- Are admin processes efficient (attendance, grading, reporting)?
    

  

- Does the school provide enough teaching resources (books, supplies, technology)?
    

  

- Does IT support respond quickly to tech issues?
    

  

- Are classrooms properly maintained?
    

  

- Is scheduling (timetables, duty rosters) fair and manageable?
    

  

- Does admin treat teachers respectfully?
    

  

- Is workload distributed fairly among staff?
    

**🎒 H. Teacher–Parent Relationship Dynamics**

  

- How often do you encounter difficult parents?
    

  

- Do parents respect your professional judgment?
    

  

- Does leadership support you when parents challenge your decisions?
    

  

- Do you feel parents understand the expectations of your subject/programme?
    

  

- Are parent expectations realistic?
    

  

- Do you feel anxious dealing with certain parents or parent groups?
    

  

- “I feel confident handling parent concerns.”
    

  

- “I feel drained when dealing with parent communication.”
    

  

This is GOLD for understanding emotional load.

  

**🏫 I. Teacher–Student Relationship & Classroom Atmosphere**

  

- Do you feel your students respect you?
    

  

- Do you feel connected to your students?
    

  

- Do you have at least one student each year who challenges your emotional resilience?
    

  

- Do students feel comfortable seeking help from you?
    

  

- Do you feel you make a positive impact?
    

  

- Are you ever afraid a student might accuse you unfairly?
    
- (VERY important — teachers never say this out loud)
    

  

- Do you feel your classroom culture is positive?
    

  

**🔥 J. School-Wide Culture, Values & Inclusion**

  

- Does the school live its stated values?
    

  

- Do teachers feel appreciated as professionals?
    

  

- Do you feel the school fosters inclusion of all backgrounds?
    

  

- Does favoritism exist among students or staff?
    

  

- Are teachers treated equally regardless of nationality?
    

  

- Do you feel safe speaking up against unfair practices?
    

  

- Is diversity respected?
    

  

This reveals systemic bias.

  

**🧘‍♂️ K. Wellbeing, Stress & Emotional Indicators**

  

- Do you feel emotionally supported by the school?
    

  

- How stressed are you on a typical week?
    

  

- How often do you feel overwhelmed?
    

  

- Do you feel this school cares about your mental health?
    

  

- “I feel valued as a teacher here.”
    

  

- “I often feel anxious coming to school.”
    

  

- “I am considering leaving at the end of this academic year.”
    

  

**🛠 L. Improvement Suggestions (Open-Ended Diagnostic)**

  

- What is ONE thing the school could improve immediately?
    

  

- What is ONE thing leadership needs to understand about teachers?
    

  

- What is ONE thing that would make your job easier?
    

  

- What is ONE cultural issue the school needs to fix?
    

  

- What is ONE thing keeping you at this school?
    

  

- What is ONE thing pushing you to consider leaving?
    

  

These are TROVES of LLM insights.
