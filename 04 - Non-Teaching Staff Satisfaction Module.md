**NON-TEACHING STAFF SATISFACTION MODULE**

  

We break down staff into typical groups:

Admin (office, finance, admissions), support staff (security, cleaners, maintenance), TAs, lab techs, transport team, cafeteria, IT.

  

But because this is FlowForge, we don’t need separate surveys — the questions cover ALL roles through universal categories.

  

**🌋 A. Role Clarity & Expectations**

  

- Is your role clearly defined?
    

  

- Do you understand what is expected of you daily?
    

  

- Are your responsibilities realistic for one person?
    

  

- Do you receive conflicting instructions from different leaders?
    

  

- Do you feel your job description matches what you actually do?
    

  

- Are procedures and protocols clearly documented?
    

  

- Do you feel your role is respected and valued in the school?
    

**🧳 B. Workload & Fairness**

  

- How manageable is your daily workload?
    

  

- Are you frequently asked to take on additional tasks without extra support?
    

  

- Are urgent requests realistic or last-minute and overwhelming?
    

  

- Is workload distributed fairly among your team?
    

  

- Do you feel overworked compared to colleagues in similar roles?
    

  

- Do you have enough staff in your department to function effectively?
    

  

- Are you often expected to work beyond regular hours?
    

  

- Do you feel your workload has increased in the past year?
    

  

**🤝 C. Staff–Staff Relationship Dynamics**

  

- Do you feel respected by teaching staff?
    

  

- Do teachers treat you as an equal professional or as “lower tier”?
    

  

- Are there staff members who make your job unnecessarily difficult?
    

  

- Do you feel appreciated by other departments?
    

  

- Does teamwork across non-teaching teams feel supportive or stressful?
    

  

- Do you feel comfortable asking colleagues for help?
    

  

- Are there cliques or favoritism that affect your work environment?
    

  

- Do you feel excluded from staff culture because of your job type or nationality?
    

  

- (VERY important in MY/SG schools where hierarchy is real.)
    

  

**👨‍💼 D. Staff–Leadership Relationship & Culture**

  

- Does leadership communicate with you respectfully?
    

  

- Do you feel leadership cares about your wellbeing?
    

  

- Are your concerns taken seriously by management?
    

  

- Do leaders treat non-teaching staff differently from teachers?
    

  

- Does leadership listen when you report problems?
    

  

- Do leaders blame staff for issues outside their control?
    

  

- Do you trust the principal / SLT to support you?
    

  

- Have you ever felt intimidated by anyone in leadership?
    

  

- This identifies TOXIC admin cultures immediately.
    

  

**👩‍💼 E. Relationship With Teachers**

  

(Teachers bully or disrespect support staff FAR more often than schools admit.)

  

- Do teachers give you reasonable requests?
    

  

- Do teachers respect your time and responsibilities?
    

  

- Have teachers spoken to you rudely or disrespectfully?
    

  

- Do teachers expect you to complete tasks outside your scope?
    

  

- Do teachers collaborate effectively with you?
    

  

- Do teachers acknowledge your contributions?
    

  

- This is GOLD because staff almost NEVER get to say this safely.
    

  

**🛠 F. Resources & Tools Provided for Work**

  

- Do you have the tools you need to perform your job?
    

  

- Are equipment, systems, or software updated and functional?
    

  

- Do you receive timely support from IT or relevant departments?
    

  

- Are physical spaces (offices, storage, work areas) adequate?
    

  

- Are safety standards followed in your work environment?
    

  

- Do you have enough training to use the systems required?
    

  

- Are you ever forced to improvise because of lack of resources?
    

**💬 G. Communication Flow & Information Access**

  

- Are you informed about school changes that impact your work?
    

  

- Do you receive information early enough to prepare for tasks?
    

  

- Do you feel “the last to know” about important events or changes?
    

  

- Are instructions from leadership clear or confusing/vague?
    

  

- Do you feel kept out of the loop compared to other departments?
    

  

- Are meetings helpful or a waste of time?
    

**💼 H. Training, Development & Career Path**

  

- Have you received any training this year relevant to your job?
    

  

- Do you want training the school hasn’t provided?
    

  

- Are there opportunities for career advancement?
    

  

- Do you have a long-term future at this school?
    

  

- Would you stay longer if training and advancement improved?
    

  

- Most support staff leave because their role is stagnant.
    

  

**🧘‍♀️ I. Respect, Recognition & School Culture**

  

- Do you feel appreciated for your contributions?
    

  

- Does the school celebrate staff achievement fairly?
    

  

- Do teachers treat you as “less important”?
    

  

- Does your nationality affect how you are treated?
    

  

- Do you feel included in the school community?
    

  

- Do you feel invisible or overlooked in staff events?
    

  

- Do you feel safe voicing your opinions?
    

  

- A LOT of hidden discrimination appears here.
    

  

**💥 J. Handling Difficult Parents / Students / Teachers**

  

- Non-teaching staff often face abuse.
    

  

- Have you been mistreated by parents while performing your role?
    

  

- Does leadership protect you when parents behave aggressively?
    

  

- Are disciplinary processes clear when students disrespect staff?
    

  

- Have you ever felt unsafe dealing with a student or parent?
    

  

- Do teachers or admin expect you to handle conflicts alone?
    

  

Some schools treat support staff like punching bags.  This exposes it.

  

**🛡 K. Safety, Security & Emotional Wellbeing**

  

- Do you feel physically safe at school?
    

  

- Do you feel emotionally safe at work?
    

  

- Do you feel overwhelmed or stressed regularly?
    

  

- Does the school check on staff wellbeing?
    

  

- Do you feel burnt out?
    

  

**🧾 L. Compensation & Job Security (sensitive questions)**

  

- Do you feel fairly compensated for your responsibilities?
    

  

- Are salary increments transparent and fair?
    

  

- Do benefits meet your needs (medical, leave, etc.)?
    

  

- Do you feel your job is secure?
    

  

- Do you consider leaving due to pay or work conditions?
    

  

- We can mark these as optional if the school chooses.
    

  

**🧩 M. Improvement, Suggestions & Open Feedback**

  

- What is one thing your department could improve?
    

  

- What is one thing the school could do to support your role better?
    

  

- What part of your job gives you the most stress?
    

  

- What part of your job brings you the most satisfaction?
    

  

- What would make you stay at this school long-term?
