**LEADERSHIP & CULTURE MODULE** 

  

This module is broken into DEEP layers that capture:

  

- system-level failures
    
- leadership blind spots
    
- culture rotation 
    
- accountability gaps
    
- communication failures
    
- favoritism and power abuse
    
- strategic incompetence
    
- HR justice
    
- relationships with staff, parents, students
    
- emotional intelligence
    

  

We’re building this EXACTLY how consulting firms evaluate CEOs — but adapted to schools.

  

**🌋 A. Vision, Direction & Strategic Clarity**

  

- Does the school have a clear mission and strategic direction?
    

  

- Is the leadership team consistent in communicating that direction?
    

  

- Do different departments (academics, admissions, pastoral, operations) share the SAME understanding of school priorities?
    

  

- Do you understand how your role contributes to the school’s long-term strategy?
    

  

- Does leadership adjust strategy based on data and reality, or feelings/politics?
    

  

- Does leadership SET priorities clearly, or create confusion?
    

  

- Does the school flip-flop direction often?
    

  

- “I trust that our leadership team knows where the school is headed.”
    

  

This exposes vision inflation, confusion, and chaos.

  

**📣 B. Communication Quality, Transparency & Authenticity**

  

- Are leadership communications clear and timely?
    

  

- Does leadership communicate difficult decisions transparently (or sugarcoat / hide details)?
    

  

- Are major decisions explained with rationale or simply announced?
    

  

- How honest do you feel leadership is with the staff?
    

  

- Do you feel leadership listens, or just broadcasts?
    

  

- Are staff consulted before decisions that affect their work?
    

  

- Does leadership consistently follow through on what they say?
    

  

- Does leadership avoid responsibility when things go wrong?
    

  

“I feel leadership communicates respectfully and authentically.”

  

**🔥 C. Leadership Competence & Decision-Making**

  

- Do you feel leadership makes sound decisions?
    

  

- Are decisions data-driven or influenced by politics/emotions?
    

  

- How often do decisions feel rushed or poorly thought out?
    

  

- Does leadership consider downstream impact when making decisions?
    

  

- Are crises handled calmly and effectively?
    

  

- Does leadership correct course when a decision proves ineffective?
    

  

- “Leadership understands the realities of the school.”
    

  

- “Leadership is competent in their roles.”
    

  

This is where weak principals get exposed.

  

**🧠 D. Emotional Intelligence & Relational Leadership**

  

- Does leadership show empathy toward staff?
    

  

- Do they handle conflict professionally?
    

  

- Are they approachable when staff need support?
    

  

- Do they treat everyone with respect, regardless of role?
    

  

- Do they apologize when they are wrong?
    

  

- Does leadership have favorites?
    

  

- Is leadership reactive or calm under pressure?
    

  

“I feel emotionally safe interacting with leadership.”

**🫅 E. Power Dynamics, Fairness & Integrity**

  

- Are staff treated equally regardless of nationality?
    

  

- Do certain staff receive special treatment?
    

  

- Do leaders enforce rules fairly or selectively?
    

  

- Are disciplinary actions consistent?
    

  

- Are promotions based on merit or politics?
    

  

- Do staff fear retaliation for giving feedback?
    

  

- Does leadership gossip or speak negatively about staff?
    

  

- “I trust leadership to act with integrity.”
    

  

THIS is one of the most important diagnostic categories.

  

**🧩 F. Leadership Support & Advocacy for Staff**

  

- Do you feel leadership stands up for staff when dealing with parents?
    

  

- Does leadership support teachers with difficult students?
    

  

- Does leadership back up non-teaching staff when mistreated?
    

  

- Does leadership take bullying or harassment issues seriously?
    

  

- Do they provide resources needed to do your job well?
    

  

- Are they proactive in removing obstacles or always reactive?
    

  

- “Leadership has my back when it matters.”
    

  

Schools lose staff when this fails.

  

**🧬 G. Culture Setting & Psychological Safety**

  

- Does leadership foster a culture of respect and collegiality?
    

  

- Are staff encouraged to share ideas without fear?
    

  

- Do staff feel safe challenging decisions?
    

  

- Does the school tolerate toxic behavior (from teachers, parents, or leaders)?
    

  

- Is there a blame culture or a learning culture?
    

  

- Do staff feel valued or replaceable?
    

  

“I feel safe being honest about my concerns.”

  

This measures CULTURE HEALTH — the hidden engine of retention.

  

**🧑‍🏫 H. Leadership’s Relationship With Teachers**

  

- Are teachers consulted before major academic changes?
    

  

- Does leadership understand the workload realities for teachers?
    

  

- Does leadership provide constructive feedback?
    

  

- Do teachers feel micromanaged or trusted?
    

  

- Are teacher voices included in school improvements?
    

  

- Does leadership handle teacher-parent conflicts fairly?
    

  

- Are teachers treated like professionals?
    

  

- We often find principals create a culture of fear.
    

  

**🛠 I. Leadership’s Relationship With Non-Teaching Staff**

  

- Are support staff treated with respect?
    

  

- Do leaders speak down to staff?
    

  

- Do staff feel invisible or undervalued?
    

  

- Are workload expectations fair for support roles?
    

  

- Does leadership intervene when teachers mistreat support staff?
    

  

- Are promotions/raises fair across staff levels?
    

  

Support staff are the MOST honest about leadership behavior.

**🎓 J. Leadership’s Relationship With Students**

  

- Is leadership visible to students?
    

  

- Do students feel comfortable approaching leaders?
    

  

- Does leadership understand student wellbeing and stress?
    

  

- Are student complaints handled with fairness?
    

  

- Does leadership ever appear disconnected from student reality?
    

  

- Students often know more than teachers about leadership morals.
    

  

**💬 K. Feedback Handling & Conflict Resolution**

  

- Does leadership invite feedback or avoid it?
    

  

- Are feedback channels safe and anonymous?
    

  

- Does leadership take action on feedback?
    

  

- Do staff fear punishment for speaking up?
    

  

- How does leadership deal with conflict — escalation or resolution?
    

  

- Do they mediate fairly between staff disputes?
    

  

Schools ALWAYS fail here.

  

**🛠 L. Change Management (CRUCIAL)**

  

- Does leadership introduce new initiatives thoughtfully?
    

  

- Do they provide enough training before implementing changes?
    

  

- Are changes planned or last-minute?
    

  

- Do staff get overwhelmed by too many new ideas?
    

  

- Do leaders evaluate impact before moving to the next initiative?
    

  

- Are changes aligned with the school’s actual needs?
    

  

Most schools suffer “initiative fatigue.”

  

**📚 M. HR Practices, Recruitment & Retention**

  

- Are hiring processes fair and transparent?
    

  

- Is teacher turnover high or low?
    

  

- Do exit interviews lead to real improvements?
    

  

- Is poor behavior from certain teachers tolerated because they “perform academically”?
    

  

- Do staff feel secure in their jobs?
    

  

- Are contracts, evaluations, and expectations clear?
    

  

This shows if the school’s people strategy is broken.

  

**📊 N. Leadership Performance & Accountability (the taboo category)**

  

- Do leaders hold themselves accountable for mistakes?
    

  

- Do they take credit for others’ work?
    

  

- Do they blame staff when things go wrong?
    

  

- Does leadership welcome external evaluations (IB/CIS/WASC) or fear them?
    

  

- Do you feel the school would improve with changes in leadership?
    

  

- If the principal left tomorrow, would the school get better, worse, or stay the same?
    

  

This question ALONE is a bombshell.

**🧘‍♀️ O. Staff Wellbeing Support from Leadership**

  

- Does leadership check in on teacher wellbeing?
    

  

- Are support staff included in wellbeing initiatives?
    

  

- Do leaders respond appropriately to burnout signals?
    

  

- Are mental health resources available?
    

  

- Do you feel leadership genuinely cares about your wellbeing?
    

  

**🧨 P. Emotional & Psychological Safety Indicators (high-signal items)**

  

- “I feel respected by leadership.”
    

  

- “I feel safe raising concerns.”
    

  

- “I trust leadership.”
    

  

- “I often feel anxious dealing with leadership.”
    

  

- “Leadership values people, not just results.”
    

  

- “I feel leadership understands the emotional climate of the school.”
    

  

These are the core diagnostic signals for leadership failure.

  

**💡 Q. Open-Ended Deep Diagnostics**

  

- What is one thing leadership is doing well?
    

  

- What is one thing leadership urgently needs to change?
    

  

- What behavior from leadership has affected you positively or negatively this year?
    

  

- What would make you trust leadership more?
    

  

- What would make you stay longer at this school?
    

  

These provide FIRE insights for the LLM to cluster themes.
