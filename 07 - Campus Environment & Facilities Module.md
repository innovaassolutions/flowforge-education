This module is going to be one of the most critical operational diagnostics, because the physical environment of a school impacts:

  

- student wellbeing
    
- teacher teaching conditions
    
- staff morale
    
- parent perception
    
- safety
    
- maintenance cost
    
- campus culture
    

  

Most schools FAIL in facilities because:

  

- maintenance is reactive, not proactive
    
- reporting is inconsistent
    
- issues take months to fix
    
- classrooms are outdated
    
- toilets are disgusting (this is the #1 parent complaint)
    
- AC breaks constantly
    
- sports areas are neglected
    
- safety hazards aren’t addressed
    
- cleaning staff is understaffed
    
- environment doesn’t match tuition fees
    
- leadership doesn’t see what staff/students experience
    

  

We’re going to build the most comprehensive and intelligence-ready Campus Environment & Facilities Module EVER.

**CAMPUS ENVIRONMENT & FACILITIES MODULE**

  

**🌋 A. Classroom Environment & Learning Spaces**

  

- Are classrooms clean and well-maintained?
    

  

- Are classroom temperatures comfortable (AC / ventilation issues)?
    

  

- Are chairs/desks adequate, stable, and comfortable?
    

  

- Are lighting levels appropriate for learning?
    

  

- Do classrooms have functioning AV / projectors / smart boards?
    

  

- Are electrical outlets safe and available?
    

  

- Do classrooms feel overcrowded or appropriately sized?
    

  

- Are learning spaces designed to support the curriculum style (IB inquiry, British structured, etc.)?
    

  

- Are classroom repairs handled promptly?
    

  

“My classroom environment supports effective teaching/learning.”

**🚻 B. Bathrooms & Hygiene Standards**

  

(This is the most sensitive yet important school facilities category.)

  

- Are toilets consistently clean?
    

  

- Are soap, tissues, and supplies replenished regularly?
    

  

- Are there foul odors or hygiene issues?
    

  

- Are facilities functional (locks, flush, taps)?
    

  

- Are bathroom areas safe and well-lit?
    

  

- Do students avoid bathrooms due to poor conditions?
    

  

“I feel comfortable using the school bathrooms.”  This is a TRUE quality indicator for schools.

  

**🏫 C. General Campus Cleanliness**

  

- Are hallways, common areas, and stairwells clean?
    

  

- Is littering a problem on campus?
    

  

- Is the cleaning staff adequate in number?
    

  

- Are spills, messes, or accidents cleaned quickly?
    

  

- Are smells (cafeteria, waste areas) managed properly?
    

  

“The school maintains a clean and welcoming environment.”

**🛠 D. Maintenance Response & Repair Efficiency**

  

Do staff know how to report maintenance issues?

  

- Are repair requests addressed within reasonable time?
    

  

- Are recurring issues fixed permanently or repeatedly patched?
    

  

- Are classrooms often left unusable or disrupted due to maintenance delays?
    

  

- Does maintenance follow safety protocols when working near students?
    

  

- Are major breakdowns handled professionally?
    

  

“Maintenance responds quickly when something breaks.”

  

“I trust the maintenance team to keep the campus safe and functional.”

  

**❄️ E. Temperature Control, Ventilation & Air Quality**

  

- Is classroom temperature consistently comfortable?
    

  

- Are ACs well-maintained and functional?
    

  

- Are there hot/cold spots in the school?
    

  

- Are windows/vents adequate for airflow?
    

  

- Is humidity managed in classrooms and storage areas?
    

  

- Are any rooms moldy or damp?
    

  

“I feel physically comfortable in most campus spaces.”

  

This affects teacher retention AND student wellbeing.

**💡 F. Lighting, Noise Levels & Acoustics**

  

- Are learning spaces well-lit?
    

  

- Is glare an issue in classrooms?
    

  

- Are hallways and common areas properly lit?
    

  

- Are classrooms soundproof enough for focused learning?
    

  

- Are noise levels manageable in key areas?
    

  

“The physical environment supports concentration.”

  

**🍽 G. Cafeteria & Dining Facilities**

  

(This ties into nutrition, hygiene, and operations.)

  

- Is the cafeteria clean and hygienic?
    

  

- Are dining areas spacious enough?
    

  

- Are queues managed well?
    

  

- Are tables/chairs clean and functional?
    

  

- Is food served at appropriate temperatures?
    

  

- Are waste bins sufficient and emptied regularly?
    

  

- Does the dining experience feel safe and pleasant?
    

  

“The cafeteria facilities meet the needs of students/staff.”

**🏋️ H. Sports, Recreation & Outdoor Areas**

  

- Are playgrounds safe and well-maintained?
    

  

- Are sports fields, courts, and swimming pools in good condition?
    

  

- Are there adequate shaded areas for students?
    

  

- Are outdoor facilities regularly inspected for hazards?
    

  

- Are PE facilities equipped and updated?
    

  

- Are changing rooms clean and functional?
    

  

“Sports facilities support a healthy student experience.”

  

**📚 I. Library, Resource Centres & Study Spaces**

  

- Are libraries inviting and properly maintained?
    

  

- Are study areas quiet and comfortable?
    

  

- Are shelves, furniture, and floors in good condition?
    

  

- Is equipment (computers, scanners) functional?
    

  

- Does the environment support focus and reading?
    

  

“I feel the library environment enhances learning.”

**🚧 J. Safety Infrastructure & Security Systems**

  

- Are emergency exits clearly marked and always accessible?
    

  

- Are stairwells, corridors, and outdoor areas safe?
    

  

- Are security cameras functioning in sensitive areas?
    

  

- Are guard stations manned adequately?
    

  

- Are parking lots safe and well-lit?
    

  

- Are drop-off/pick-up zones safe?
    

  

“I feel safe moving around the campus.”

  

**🪑 K. Furniture, Equipment & Material Quality**

  

- Are tables and chairs stable and comfortable?
    

  

- Do labs have functional equipment?
    

  

- Are storage areas adequate?
    

  

- Do classrooms have enough materials for learning?
    

  

- Is equipment replaced proactively or only when broken?
    

  

**🧼 L. Cleaning Staff Capacity & Quality**

  

- Are there enough cleaners for the size of campus?
    

  

- Are cleaning standards consistent throughout the day?
    

  

- Are cleaners treated respectfully by school community?
    

  

- Are deep-cleaning tasks done regularly?
    

  

“Cleaning staff contributes positively to the campus environment.”

  

**🗂 M. Campus Accessibility & Navigability**

  

- Is it easy for new students/parents to navigate campus?
    

  

- Are signs clear and sufficient?
    

  

- Are elevators/ramps accessible for individuals with disabilities?
    

  

- Are blocked pathways/common obstructions an issue?
    

**🎨 N. Aesthetic Quality, Ambience & School Identity**

  

- Does the campus feel inspiring or outdated?
    

  

- Are classrooms decorated intentionally (not cluttered/dull)?
    

  

- Is the school brand identity visible in the environment?
    

  

- Does the campus environment reflect the tuition fees?
    

  

- “The campus environment contributes positively to school pride.”
    

  

Parents CARE about this in premium-fee schools.

  

**⭐ O. Emotional & Psychological Indicators of Facilities Experience**

  

- “The physical environment makes me feel comfortable being on campus.”
    

  

- “The campus looks and feels like a high-quality school.”
    

  

- “I sometimes feel embarrassed bringing guests to the school.”
    

  

- “The environment improves my mood and motivation.”
    

  

- “I feel the school invests properly in facilities upkeep.”
    

  

This uncovers VALUE PERCEPTION — a major driver of enrollment decisions.

  

**🧩 P. Open-Ended Insight Questions**

  

- What facility or area needs urgent improvement?
    

  

- What part of the campus do you enjoy most?
    

  

- Which maintenance issue has bothered you the most this year?
    

  

- What would make the campus feel safer and more welcoming?
    

  

- What facilities investment would have the biggest impact on school quality?
