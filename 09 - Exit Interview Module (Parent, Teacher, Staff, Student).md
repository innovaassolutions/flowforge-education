
This is one of the MOST IMPORTANT modules in the ENTIRE FlowForge ecosystem because Exit Interviews are the single most high-signal diagnostic tool any school has… and almost NO school uses them properly.

  

We’re going to build a multi-stakeholder EXIT INTERVIEW MODULE so comprehensive that FlowForge can:

  

- detect hidden culture issues
    
- identify who is causing people to leave (leaders, teachers, departments, policies)
    
- reveal patterns in WHY people leave
    
- expose avoidable losses
    
- map push vs pull factors
    
- detect retention risk in the CURRENT community
    
- strengthen institutional memory
    
- diagnose internal toxicity
    
- help SLT see problems BEFORE they turn into resignations or withdrawals
    

  

This is the “black box” of school truth.  We’re opening it.  

  

Let’s go.

  

⭐ **WHO TAKES THIS MODULE?**

  

Exit reasons differ by stakeholder.

We want the LLM to triangulate patterns AND contradictions.

  

1. Parents (withdrawing children)

Because they reveal:

  

- academic dissatisfaction
    
- wellbeing issues
    
- unsafe environment
    
- weak leadership credibility
    
- curriculum mismatch
    
- operational failure(s)
    
- communication breakdown
    
- teacher issues
    
- peer issues
    
- fees vs value gap
    

  

Parents give the MOST honest feedback because they no longer fear consequences.

  

2. Teachers (resigning/relocating)

  

Because they reveal:

  

- toxic leadership
    
- workload burnout
    
- unfair treatment
    
- staff culture issues
    
- lack of support
    
- student behavior management
    
- low pay/value mismatch
    
- PD/career stagnation
    
- poor HR processes
    

  

Teachers rarely speak truth unless they’re exiting — this is GOLD.

  

3. Non-Teaching Staff

  

Because they reveal:

  

- admin oppression
    
- unfair treatment
    
- pay/role mismatch
    
- no development opportunities
    
- leadership disrespect
    
- poor operational culture
    

  

Support staff is the LEAST protected group — they often know the truth that teachers don’t.

  

4. Students (age 12+)

  

Because they reveal:

  

- bullying
    
- discrimination
    
- academic stress
    
- teacher relationships
    
- social exclusion
    
- curriculum misalignment
    
- mental health risks
    
- community culture
    

  

Older students are INCREDIBLY perceptive about leadership, teachers, and peer toxicity.

  

5. Leadership (self-assessment)

  

Because they reveal:

  

- whether the SLT recognizes their own systemic failures
    
- alignment between principal, deputies, heads of school
    
- blind spots
    
- excuses
    
- culture mindset
    

  

Leadership self-report vs community report becomes one of the strongest diagnostic signals.

  

We’re going to break this into DEEP diagnostic categories + stakeholder variations.

  

**🌋 A. Reason for Leaving (Push vs Pull)**

**ALL stakeholders**

  

- What are the MAIN reasons for leaving the school?
    

  

- Was this decision driven more by problems here (PUSH) or opportunities elsewhere (PULL)?
    

  

- Did one specific incident trigger the decision?
    

  

- How long have you been considering leaving?
    

  

- Was the reason personal, academic, professional, or institutional?
    

  

- If multiple reasons, which was the PRIMARY one?
    

  

- LLM will cluster patterns across groups.
    

**📚 B. Academic Experience & Curriculum Fit**

**Parents + Students + Teachers**

  

**Parents:**

  

- Did you feel the curriculum met your child’s needs?
    

  

- Was academic rigor too weak, too strong, or appropriate?
    

  

- Did you feel confident in teacher quality?
    

  

- Did assessments align with expectations?
    

  

- Did curriculum choice influence your departure?
    

  

**Students (12+):**

  

- Did you feel stressed or overwhelmed academically?
    

  

- Did you feel supported by teachers?
    

  

- Did you find classes engaging?
    

  

- Did you struggle due to teaching style or curriculum demands?
    

  

**Teachers:**

  

- Was curriculum delivery workload reasonable?
    

  

- Did leadership support your instructional needs?
    

  

- Were academic expectations unrealistic?
    

  

**🧠 C. Wellbeing, Safety & Pastoral Care**

**Parents + Students + Teachers**

**Parents:**

  

- Did your child feel safe at school?
    

  

- Was bullying handled effectively?
    

  

- Was wellbeing support adequate?
    

  

**Students:**

  

- Did you feel safe emotionally and physically?
    

  

- Did bullying or social exclusion play a role in leaving?
    

  

- Did you have a trusted adult at school?
    

  

**Teachers:**

  

- Did student behavior contribute to burnout?
    

  

- Were you supported during conflict situations?
    

  

**👥 D. Relationships With Key Stakeholders**

**Tailored for all groups**

**Parents → Teacher/Leadership/Admin**

  

- Were teacher relationships positive?
    

  

- Did admin treat you respectfully?
    

  

- Did leadership respond adequately to concerns?
    

  

- Teachers → Leadership/Colleagues/Parents
    

  

- Did leadership treat you fairly?
    

  

- Did colleagues support you?
    

  

- Did parent behavior contribute to your decision?
    

  

**Staff → Teachers/Admin/Leadership**

  

- Did you feel respected by teachers?
    

  

- Did leadership treat you as an equal?
    

  

- Were you ever mistreated by parents?
    

  

- Students → Teachers/Peers/Leadership
    

  

- Were teacher interactions supportive?
    

  

- Did peer relationships influence departure?
    

  

- Did leadership make school better or worse?
    

  

**🔥 E. Culture, Inclusion & Belonging**

**All stakeholders**

  

- Did you feel welcomed and valued?
    

  

- Did you experience any form of discrimination?
    

  

- Did you feel included in the school community?
    

  

- Was the school culture improving or declining?
    

  

- Was there favoritism or unfair treatment?
    

  

- 🧩 F. Leadership Credibility & Trust
    
- Teachers + Staff + Parents + Students (12+)
    

  

- Did leadership influence your decision to leave?
    

  

- Did you trust leadership decisions?
    

  

- Was the school’s direction clear and consistent?
    

  

- Did leadership intervene effectively when issues arose?
    

  

- Did leadership acknowledge concerns or ignore them?
    

  

**🛠 G. Operational, Administrative & Facility Pain Points**

**Parents + Teachers + Staff**

**Parents:**

  

- Did operational issues (transport, billing, comms) push you to leave?
    

  

**Teachers:**

  

- Did admin inefficiency add to your workload?
    

  

**Staff:**

  

- Did facilities or operations cause daily stress?
    

  

**⚖️ H. Value Perception (Parents)**

**Parents only**

  

- Did you feel the school delivered value for the tuition paid?
    

  

- Did fee increases feel justified?
    

  

- Did facilities match expectations?
    

  

- Did value perception influence the decision to leave?
    

  

**🧬 I. HR, Contract, & Career Issues (Teachers & Staff)**

  

- Did pay/benefits influence your decision to leave?
    

  

- Were contract expectations clear?
    

  

- Did you feel your role had growth opportunities?
    

  

- Did leadership support your development?
    

  

- Did HR treat you fairly and consistently?
    

  

**🧘‍♀️ J. Emotional & Psychological Signals**

**ALL groups**

  

- “I felt anxious/uncomfortable interacting with leadership/teachers/staff.”
    

  

- “I did not feel seen or valued.”
    

  

- “I often felt stressed or overwhelmed at school.”
    

  

- “I lost trust in the school’s environment/culture.”
    

  

- “I am relieved to be leaving.”
    

  

- “I am sad to be leaving.”
    

  

- “I would return if things changed.”
    

  

**⭐ K. Retention Insight (“Could this have been prevented?”)**

**ALL except students under 12**

  

- Could the school have done anything to keep you?
    

  

- What SINGLE change would have made you stay?
    

  

- What was the REAL tipping point?
    

  

- Would you consider returning in the future?
    

  

- Would you recommend this school to others after leaving?
    

  

**🧾 L. Open-Ended Diagnostic Questions**

  

- What was the BEST part of your experience at this school?
    

  

- What was the WORST part?
    

  

- What would you want leadership to know about your time here?
    

  

- What suggestions do you have for improvement?
