Schools almost NEVER face this honestly.  FlowForge will.

  

**WHO TAKES THIS MODULE?**

**Parents**

  

Because they judge:

  

- child’s safety
    
- bullying handling
    
- communication
    
- wellbeing support
    
- trust in leadership
    
- pastoral care quality
    
- Students (Age 8–11: simplified / Age 12+: full version)
    

  

Because they ARE the victims, witnesses, and participants in the social ecosystem.  Students know the REAL TRUTH.

  

**Teachers**

Because they see:

  

- teacher-to-student boundaries
    
- peer incidents
    
- bullying
    
- pastoral failures
    
- child protection gaps
    
- unsafe classrooms
    

**Non-Teaching Staff**

Because:

  

- they witness behavior around playgrounds, corridors, cafeterias
    
- students confide in them informally
    
- they often see things teachers/leadership don’t
    

  

**Leadership/SLT**

Because they must evaluate:

  

- procedures
    
- readiness
    
- policy implementation
    
- team capacity
    
- reporting systems
    
- safeguarding culture warmth
    

  

Every stakeholder must answer DIFFERENT questions — to triangulate risk.

  

**1. PARENTS — Exit Interview Safeguarding & Pastoral Care Questions**

**A. Child Safety Perception**

  

- Does your child feel safe at school?
    

  

- Has your child ever been bullied at this school?
    

  

- How effectively did the school address bullying incidents?
    

  

- Do you feel confident that your child is protected during school hours?
    

  

- Were there any safety concerns that influenced your decision to leave?
    

  

**B. Communication & Response**

  

- Did the school inform you of safety issues promptly?
    

  

- Did the school respond quickly to your concerns about wellbeing?
    

  

- Did you feel listened to when raising pastoral concerns?
    

  

- Did leadership handle safeguarding issues professionally?
    

  

**C. Trust in Pastoral Systems**

  

- Do you trust school counselors?
    

  

- Do you trust class teachers to manage emotional struggles?
    

  

- Do you trust leadership to prioritize student safety above image/PR?
    

  

**D. Emotional Support**

  

- Did the school support your child emotionally when needed?
    

  

- Were wellbeing programs effective for your child?
    

  

- Did your child feel they had a safe adult to confide in?
    

  

**E. Open Feedback**

  

- What is the biggest safeguarding/pastoral weakness you observed?
    

  

- What did the school do well in terms of emotional support?
    

  

- What needs urgent improvement?
    

  

**2. STUDENTS (AGE 12+) — Safeguarding & Pastoral Care Questions**

**A. Safety & Bullying**

  

- Do you feel safe at school?
    

  

- Have you ever been bullied (in person/online)?
    

  

- Did you report it?
    

  

- Did anyone help you?
    

  

- Are you afraid of any student(s)?
    

  

- Are you afraid of any teacher or staff member?
    

  

**B. Adult Behavior**

  

- Has any teacher/staff spoken to you in a way that made you uncomfortable?
    

  

- Has any adult in school crossed personal boundaries?
    

  

- Do you trust teachers to handle issues fairly?
    

  

- Have you ever avoided a teacher because of how they treat students?
    

**C. Reporting Channels**

  

- Do you know HOW to report bullying or safety concerns?
    

  

- Do you trust the reporting system?
    

  

- Are you afraid of retaliation if you report something?
    

  

- Do teachers believe you when you report things?
    

**D. Emotional Wellbeing**

  

- Do you have at least one adult at school you can talk to?
    

  

- Do you feel supported when you’re stressed or upset?
    

  

- Does the school handle mental-health crises well?
    

  

- Do teachers notice when you’re not doing well?
    

  

**E. Peers**

  

- Do students treat each other respectfully?
    

  

- Is there racism, bullying, exclusion or cliques?
    

  

- Do you feel like you belong here?
    

  

**F. Open Feedback**

  

- What made school feel unsafe or uncomfortable?
    

  

- What would make students feel safer?
    

  

**3. TEACHERS — Safeguarding & Pastoral Diagnostic Questions**

**A. Classroom Safety**

  

- Do students feel safe in your classroom?
    

  

- Do you witness bullying incidents?
    

  

- Are you supported when handling behavior issues?
    

**B. Leadership & Reporting**

  

- Are child protection concerns taken seriously by leadership?
    

  

- Is reporting streamlined or complicated?
    

  

- Have you ever seen leadership ignore or downplay a safeguarding issue?
    

  

- Do you feel safe reporting unsafe adult behavior?
    

  

**C. Boundaries & Professional Conduct**

  

- Have you witnessed any teacher crossing boundaries with students?
    

  

- Are adults trained adequately in safeguarding?
    

  

- Do teachers understand appropriate contact protocols?
    

  

**D. Training & Preparedness**

  

- How confident do you feel handling safeguarding situations?
    

  

- Is safeguarding training useful, or a tick-box exercise?
    

  

- Do pastoral staff respond quickly when needed?
    

  

**E. Students At-Risk**

  

- Are at-risk students monitored effectively?
    

  

- Do counselors share enough information with teachers?
    

  

- Are wellbeing concerns actioned quickly?
    

  

**F. Open Feedback**

  

- What is the biggest pastoral weakness in the school?
    

  

- What needs urgent improvement?
    

  

**4. NON-TEACHING STAFF — Safeguarding & Pastoral Questions**

**A. Safety & Observation**

  

- Do you witness student conflict or bullying in your work areas?
    

  

- Are you comfortable reporting your concerns?
    

  

- Do students treat you respectfully?
    

  

- Do you see unsafe behavior regularly (running in halls, aggression, disrespect)?
    

  

**B. Leadership Response**

  

- Does leadership take your safeguarding concerns seriously?
    

  

- Are your reports acted upon promptly?
    

  

- Do teachers collaborate with you regarding student safety?
    

  

**C. Training & Awareness**

  

- Have you received safeguarding training?
    

  

- Do you understand how to respond to child protection signals?
    

  

**D. Open Feedback**

  

- What’s the biggest safety issue you observe?
    

  

- What support do YOU need to help keep students safe?
    

  

**5. LEADERSHIP — Safeguarding Self-Assessment**

**A. Systems & Procedures**

  

- Are safeguarding procedures documented clearly?
    

  

- Are reporting channels fast and confidential?
    

  

- Are follow-ups tracked and audited?
    

  

**B. Response Quality**

  

- Does the school respond quickly during safeguarding incidents?
    

  

- Are parents informed promptly and appropriately?
    

  

- Are actions consistent across cases?
    

**C. Staff Training & Culture**

  

- Do staff take safeguarding seriously?
    

  

- Is pastoral care a respected division or pushed aside?
    

  

- Do teachers feel confident escalating concerns?
    

  

**D. Leadership Reflection**

  

- What were the biggest safeguarding gaps this year?
    

  

- Where did leadership fail to respond effectively?
    

  

- What improvements are planned?
