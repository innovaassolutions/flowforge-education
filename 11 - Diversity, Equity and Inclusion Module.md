This is another HIGH-IMPACT, HIGH-SENSITIVITY module because DEI issues are often:

  

- unspoken,
    
- denied by leadership,
    
- felt strongly by students, parents, and staff,
    

  

and a MAJOR reason behind withdrawals, resignations, bullying, and culture rot.

  

**WHO TAKES THIS MODULE?**

  

Because DEI is perceived VERY differently across the community:

  

**Parents**

- They evaluate fairness, equal opportunity, teacher treatment, and whether their child feels included.
    
- Students (Age 8–11 simplified, Age 12+ full)
    
- They experience racial dynamics, social hierarchies, cliques, exclusion, bullying, identity safety.
    

  

**Teachers**

They observe unequal treatment, leadership bias, favoritism, discrimination, parental bias, and culture cracks.  They must self-reflect on DEI policies, bias incidents, reporting, training, culture.

  

- Non-Teaching Staff
    
- Often the group MOST subjected to discrimination, class bias, nationality bias, or cultural disrespect.
    
- Leadership/SLT
    

  

We TRIANGULATE differences to identify deep patterns.

  

**1. PARENTS — DEI Module Questions**

**A. Child Experience & Belonging**

  

- Does your child feel accepted at school?
    

  

- Has your child ever experienced exclusion due to race/culture/language/identity?
    

  

- Does your child feel represented and respected?
    

  

- Do classmates treat your child equally?
    

  

**B. Teacher & Staff Behavior**

  

- Do teachers treat all students fairly and without bias?
    

  

- Have you observed or sensed favoritism based on race, nationality, or status?
    

  

- Do teachers understand and respect cultural differences?
    

  

- Does your child feel safe raising concerns about unfair treatment?
    

  

- C. School Culture & Inclusion
    

  

- Does the school promote inclusivity in events, curriculum, and activities?
    

  

- Are diverse family backgrounds acknowledged and respected?
    

  

- Are school celebrations inclusive (not tokenistic)?
    

  

- Does the school foster respectful interactions across cultures?
    

**D. Communication & Representation**

  

- Does school communication reflect diverse families?
    

  

- Does the school avoid cultural stereotypes?
    

  

- Are parent-facing staff respectful to all families regardless of nationality or socioeconomic background?
    

  

**E. Open-Ended**

  

- What DEI challenges did your family experience?
    

  

- What should the school improve to be more inclusive?
    

  

**2. STUDENTS (AGE 12+) — DEI Module Questions**

**A. Identity Safety**

  

- Do you feel safe being yourself at school?
    

  

- Have you ever been teased or excluded because of your race, nationality, language, religion, or identity?
    

  

- Do you feel comfortable sharing parts of your culture with classmates?
    

  

**B. Peer Culture & Cliques**

  

- Are students respectful of differences?
    

  

- Are there cliques that exclude certain groups?
    

  

- Do you notice racial or national grouping (segregation)?
    

  

- Have you seen discrimination between students?
    

  

- Do you feel pressured to “fit in” culturally?
    

  

**C. Teacher Behavior**

  

- Do teachers treat all students equally?
    

  

- Have you seen teachers show favoritism?
    

  

- Have teachers ever made comments that felt biased or insensitive?
    

  

- Do teachers intervene when discriminatory behavior happens?
    

  

**D. School Environment**

  

- Do school events represent your culture/background?
    

  

- Does the curriculum include multiple perspectives?
    

  

- Do you feel your culture is respected and visible?
    

  

**E. Open-Ended**

  

- What made you feel included?
    

  

- What made you feel excluded?
    

  

- What DEI improvements would help students most?
    

  

**3. TEACHERS — DEI Module Questions**

**A. Staff Treatment & Equality**

  

- Are teachers treated equally regardless of nationality?
    

  

- Does leadership show favoritism based on race or background?
    

  

- Do certain nationalities receive better contracts/benefits?
    

  

- Are discipline policies applied fairly across students?
    

  

- Do you feel respected by parents from all backgrounds?
    

  

**B. Student Inclusion**

  

- Are students treated fairly regardless of race or culture?
    

  

- Are minority students monitored and supported appropriately?
    

  

- Are bullying incidents with ethnic/cultural elements handled seriously?
    

  

**C. Curriculum & Classroom Practices**

  

Does the curriculum reflect diverse perspectives?

  

Are teaching materials inclusive and respectful?

  

Do teachers receive training to avoid bias in instruction?

  

**D. Colleague Relationships**

  

- Is your department inclusive of different nationalities?
    

  

- Are you treated differently by colleagues due to nationality or background?
    

  

- Do cultural misunderstandings occur frequently?
    

  

**E. Leadership DEI Impact**

  

- Does leadership demonstrate awareness of DEI issues?
    

  

- Do you feel safe reporting discrimination?
    

  

- Has leadership ever ignored a DEI-related concern?
    

  

**F. Open-Ended**

  

- What DEI issues exist among staff?
    

  

- What would you change to create a more inclusive environment?
    

  

**4. NON-TEACHING STAFF — DEI Module Questions**

  

This is CRITICAL — support staff are often treated WORSE than teachers.

  

A. Respect & Equality

  

- Do students treat you respectfully?
    

  

- Do teachers treat you respectfully?
    

  

- Do leaders treat you respectfully?
    

  

- Have you ever felt looked down upon because of your job role?
    

  

- Have you ever been treated differently because of your nationality?
    

  

B. Inclusion in School Culture

  

- Do you feel included in staff events and meetings?
    

  

- Are cleaners/security/cafeteria/admin treated equally as “part of the school”?
    

  

- Do you feel excluded from the “teacher world” or “leadership world”?
    

  

C. Professional Treatment

  

- Do teachers or leadership give disrespectful or condescending instructions?
    

  

- Have you experienced discrimination from parents?
    

  

- Are your concerns taken seriously when reported?
    

  

D. Open-Ended

  

- Describe any discrimination you’ve experienced.
    

  

- What would help you feel more respected and included?
    

  

**🧭 5. LEADERSHIP / SLT — DEI Self-Assessment**

**A. Policy & Framework**

  

- Does the school have a DEI policy?
    

  

- Is it communicated and enforced?
    

  

- Are staff trained regularly on DEI issues?
    

  

**B. Culture Review**

  

- Are there disparities between teacher nationalities?
    

  

- Do staff or parents complain about bias?
    

  

- Are some groups favored in hiring/promotion?
    

  

- Does the student body show signs of social segregation?
    

**C. Response Effectiveness**

  

- Are DEI complaints addressed quickly and fairly?
    

  

- Are bullying incidents with cultural elements treated seriously?
    

  

- Have there been DEI violations that were mishandled?
    

  

**D. Leadership Reflection**

  

- What are our DEI blind spots?
    

  

- What major DEI improvements are needed this year?
    

  

- How does our school culture compare to our stated values?
