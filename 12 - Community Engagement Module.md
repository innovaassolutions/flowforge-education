Organisational Psychology + Parent Engagement + Student Experience

  

The Community Engagement Module is the emotional heartbeat of a school.

It measures:

  

- How connected families feel
    
- How respected alumni feel
    
- How valued teachers feel
    
- How integrated the school is with its community
    
- How active, vibrant, and inclusive school life is
    
- How well leadership fosters belonging
    
- How the school’s social ecosystem supports the mission
    

  

This is CRITICAL for:

  

✨ retention

✨ enrollment

✨ reputation

✨ culture

✨ school “vibe” (parents REALLY care about this)

  

**WHO TAKES THIS MODULE?**

  

Because “community” is multi-layered, we include:

  

**Parents**

They judge:

  

- inclusivity
    
- parent networks
    
- communication
    
- representation
    
- school events
    
- sense of belonging
    
- PTAs / parent councils
    

  

**Students (Age 8–11 simplified; 12+ full)**

They judge:

  

- peer inclusivity
    
- sense of belonging
    
- school spirit
    
- student leadership
    
- bullying vs acceptance
    
- feeling part of community
    

  

**Teachers**

They judge:

  

- staff culture
    
- parent-school relationships
    
- student engagement
    
- event participation
    
- leadership support
    
- Non-Teaching Staff
    

  

They see:

- student interactions
    
- parent behavior
    
- teacher behavior
    
- cultural dynamics
    
- campus tone
    

  

**Leadership**

They evaluate:

- community alignment
    
- school-wide participation
    
- parental trust
    
- culture-building effectiveness
    

  

This module is a cross-stakeholder triangulation of community health.

  

**PARENTS — Community Engagement Questions**

**A. Sense of Belonging**

  

- Do you feel welcomed by the parent community?
    

  

- Do you feel comfortable interacting with other parents?
    

  

- Do you feel included in school life?
    

  

- Does the school culture make you feel part of something meaningful?
    

**B. School–Parent Relationship**

  

- Do you feel the school listens to the parent community?
    

  

- Does leadership create opportunities for parent contribution?
    

  

- Are parents encouraged to collaborate, not compete?
    

  

- Do you feel respected by teachers/admin during interactions?
    

  

**C. Events & Participation**

  

- Are school events inclusive and accessible?
    

  

- Are event schedules parent-friendly?
    

  

- Are diverse cultures represented in school events?
    

  

- Are school events meaningful or superficial/PR?
    

  

**D. PTA / Parent Council**

  

- Is the parent council representative of the community?
    

  

- Is it cliquey or open to new parents?
    

  

- Does it have real influence or symbolic?
    

  

- Does PTA communicate transparently?
    

  

**E. Parent Community Dynamics**

  

- Are parent groups welcoming or divided?
    

  

- Are there cliques based on nationality or socioeconomic status?
    

  

- Have you felt judged or excluded?
    

  

- Do parents help each other or compete?
    

  

**F. Open Feedback**

  

- What is the best part of the parent community?
    

  

- What needs improvement?
    

  

- What made you feel like you belonged or didn’t belong?
    

  

**STUDENTS — Community Engagement Questions (Age 12+)**

**A. Belonging**

  

- Do you feel like you belong at this school?
    

  

- Do you feel accepted by other students?
    

  

- Do you have close friends or a support group?
    

  

- B. Student Culture
    

  

- Are students respectful across cultures?
    

  

- Are there harmful cliques?
    

  

- Are new students welcomed easily?
    

  

- Do you feel left out of activities or groups?
    

**C. Student Voice & Leadership**

  

- Do student councils represent the student body?
    

  

- Are student voices heard?
    

  

- Do you feel leadership listens to student feedback?
    

  

**D. Activities & Engagement**

  

- Do you enjoy school events?
    

  

- Are clubs and activities inclusive?
    

  

- Are activities accessible to all students?
    

  

**E. Open Feedback**

  

- What makes school feel like a community?
    

  

- What breaks community?
    

  

- How could school spirit improve?
    

  

 **TEACHERS — Community Engagement Questions**

**A. School Culture**

  

- Does the school foster a warm and inclusive environment?
    

  

- Do you feel connected to your colleagues?
    

  

- Does leadership support community-building initiatives?
    

  

**B. Teacher–Parent Interaction**

  

- Are parents respectful during interactions?
    

  

- Do parents collaborate or create divisions?
    

  

- Do teachers feel supported during parent-facing events?
    

  

**C. School-Wide Participation**

  

- Are teachers expected to attend too many events?
    

  

- Do teachers feel involved in planning community initiatives?
    

  

- Are school events meaningful and aligned with culture goals?
    

  

**D. Inter-Department Community**

  

- Is collaboration strong across departments?
    

  

- Do teachers socialize and bond meaningfully?
    

  

- Are cultural differences respected among staff?
    

  

**E. Open Feedback**

  

- What strengthens community?
    

  

- What harms staff community spirit?
    

  

**NON-TEACHING STAFF — Community Engagement Questions**

**A. Respect & Inclusion**

  

- Do parents and students treat you respectfully?
    

  

- Are you included in staff community events?
    

  

- Do teachers treat support staff as equals?
    

**B. Daily Interactions**

  

- Do you feel comfortable interacting with families?
    

  

- Are you ever ignored or dismissed during interactions?
    

  

- Does your work feel appreciated by the broader community?
    

  

**C. School Environment**

  

- Does the school feel friendly and welcoming?
    

  

- Are student behaviors respectful in common spaces?
    

  

- Is staff culture warm or segmented (teachers vs staff)?
    

  

**D. Open Feedback**

  

- What would make you feel more included?
    

  

- What community issues do you observe most?
    

  

**LEADERSHIP — Community Engagement Self-Evaluation**

**A. Community Alignment**

  

- Does the school culture match the school’s stated mission?
    

  

- Are events and initiatives inclusive of all cultures?
    

  

- Does the parent community trust the school?
    

**B. Engagement Quality**

  

- Are parent relationships healthy or strained?
    

  

- Are community concerns handled consistently?
    

  

- Are students actively engaged in school life?
    

**C. Inclusivity Practices**

  

- Are new families integrated well?
    

  

- Do student leaders represent diverse voices?
    

  

- Are community activities aligned with school values?
    

  

**D. Leadership Reflection**

  

- What are our community blind spots?
    

  

- What community-building efforts failed?
    

  

- What must improve next year?
